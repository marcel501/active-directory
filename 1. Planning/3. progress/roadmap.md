# Operating systems

Comprehensive overview of OSes its architecture, underlying systems and their administration.




## Introduction

- Firstly make familiarity of what is an OS, its essential purposes
- Briefly explore its background and its evolution
- Take interest in the boot cycle



### Hardware systems

- Investigate the computer as a whole
- Describe computer hardware
- Focus on pre-OS boot cycle
- Indicate the processes such as registers, voltage rails, sleep states, etc
- Describe the purposes of 1st stage bootloaders, POST and error codes.



### Software systems

- Investigate what a program and software is
- Focus on OS boot cycle following the 1st stage bootloader
- Explore the primary elements of OSes: Kernels, filesystems, networking, IO, etc
- Investigate the kernel: analyse the difference between users-pace and kernel-space
- Investigate filesystems
- Investigate process management: system services
- Analyse package management
- Describe system logging and configuration
